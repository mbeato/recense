Two coordinated edits to `src/source/strip-quoted.ts`. Both are required — either alone leaves a
reachable non-idempotent case.

(1) **Indent tolerance on all four line regexes.** Replace the column-0 / `{0,3}` anchors with
`[ \t]*` (arbitrary horizontal whitespace, NOT `\s*` — `\s` matches `\n` and would let a pattern
straddle the two-line attribution window):
  - `QUOTE_LINE_RE` becomes `/^[ \t]*>.*$/gm`
  - `ATTRIBUTION_RE` becomes `/^[ \t]*On [^\n]{0,200}(?:\n[^\n]{0,200})? wrote:\s*$/gim`
  - `FORWARD_MARKER_RE` gains `[ \t]*` immediately after `^`, alternation body unchanged
  - `SIGNATURE_DELIM_RE` becomes `/^[ \t]*-- $/gm`
Keep every existing flag exactly as-is. Keep the bounded `[^\n]{0,200}` spans — they are the
catastrophic-backtracking guard and must not become `.*`.

(2) **Whitespace-preserving trim.** In step 4, replace the final `result.trim()` with: map each
kept line through a trailing-horizontal-whitespace strip (`line.replace(/[ \t\r]+$/, '')`), then
drop leading and trailing lines that are now empty (advance a `start` index while the line is
`''`, retreat an `end` index likewise), then `slice(start, end).join('\n')`, then apply the
existing `EXCESS_BLANK_LINES_RE` collapse. The surviving lines' LEADING whitespace is preserved.
Keep the existing monotonicity safety net (`result.length <= text.length ? ... : slice`) unchanged.

Why (2) is load-bearing and not cosmetic: the old `.trim()` could shift an indented line to
column 0, manufacturing a fresh boundary/quote context that only a SECOND application would see —
that is the mechanical cause of the idempotence breach. With leading whitespace preserved and the
regexes indent-agnostic, pass 2 sees the same lines with the same indent and can match nothing new,
so idempotence follows structurally rather than by fixture coverage. Trailing-whitespace stripping
cannot create a new match either: `ATTRIBUTION_RE` and `FORWARD_MARKER_RE` already end in `\s*$`,
`QUOTE_LINE_RE` ends in `.*$`, and `SIGNATURE_DELIM_RE` requires a trailing space that trimming
only ever removes.

Update the doc comments in the SAME edit so they stay honest — a stale comment here is what the
review flagged elsewhere:

    soft-wrap of quoted lines)" -> any leading horizontal whitespace is tolerated.
  - the `ATTRIBUTION_RE` docblock's "Anchored at line start" -> anchored at line start after
    optional leading horizontal whitespace.
  - the algorithm block's step 4 (line ~102) "and `.trim()` the result" -> describe the
    blank-line/trailing-whitespace trim and state that leading indentation of surviving lines is
    preserved deliberately.
  - add one line to the Contract block's **Idempotent** bullet naming why it now holds
    structurally (indent-agnostic matching + a trim that never re-anchors a line to column 0),
    citing 65-REVIEW WR-03.

Do NOT touch `EXCESS_BLANK_LINES_RE` or its comment (that is IN-03, a separate finding). Do NOT
touch `isNearEmptyResidual`, the over-cap guard, or the two-line attribution window (IN-04). Do
