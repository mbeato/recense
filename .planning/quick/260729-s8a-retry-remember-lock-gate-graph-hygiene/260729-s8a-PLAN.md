<objective>
Fix the two causes of `recense remember` write-lock contention observed live today.

**Cause A — remember fast-fails.** `remember-cli.ts:427` uses bare `acquireLock()` and exits in 0.11s the moment anything else holds the lock. `recall-cli.ts:126` solved the identical problem with `acquireLockWithRetry()` and documents the reasoning; that reasoning was never carried across to remember.

**Cause B — hygiene runs every turn.** `stop-cli.ts` is the Claude Code Stop hook ("fires per turn") and spawns a detached sleep pass on every turn. `run-sleep-pass.ts:742` calls `runGraphHygiene` on EVERY such pass while `RECENSE_SLEEP_DEDUP=1` (set in sleep.env since Jul 2). That function does a `VACUUM INTO` of the whole 316 MB DB plus entity+fact dedup over ~25k nodes / ~25k edges — **measured at 486 seconds**, holding the write lock the entire time. So remember collides with a ~8-minute lock hold many times a day.

Purpose: make curated `recense remember` writes reliable during normal Claude Code use, without weakening the single-writer invariant, without re-adding anything that already exists (snapshot retention `SNAP_KEEP=5`, O_EXCL lockfile, `LOCK_STALE_MS`, `startLockHeartbeat`), and without touching `RECENSE_SLEEP_DEDUP` or the user's sleep.env.

Output: a retry budget at the remember call site, a meta-persisted interval gate on graph hygiene, vitest coverage for both, and a rebuilt `dist/`.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-s8a-02 | Denial of Service | `remember` blocking an agent turn | accept | Worst case a curated write waits ~10 min behind a stuck pass; Fix B removes the routine 8-minute hold that made this common, and losing a curated fact silently is the worse failure.
