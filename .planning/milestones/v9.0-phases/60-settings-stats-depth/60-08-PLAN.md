"is this normal?" and "did that cost lever work?" without hand-querying the ledger. These are
locked founder decisions (2026-07-11).

`by_feature`, `by_model`, `retail_usd`, and `cost_event_deltas` (chart-marker deltas) are
UNCHANGED — this plan only ADDS fields.

provider quota data. Do NOT fabricate a "quota remaining" number. Framing is "vs your typical".
