<objective>
Close the three confirmed predicate-drift defects (WR-01, WR-03, WR-04) surfaced by 61-REVIEW.md
and re-confirmed in 61-VERIFICATION.md. All three share one root cause: `index.js`, `corpus.js`,
and `server.ts` maintain three separately hand-written definitions of "what is a recognized
project" and "who owns focus state," and they silently disagree in reachable configurations
(hub-only projects, scope-less docs, unresolved schema roots with children).

The fix UNIFIES the definition rather than point-patching three call sites: the server becomes the
single source of the recognized-project set (shipped on `/graph?type=doc`), the client consumes it,
the null-safety guards are added to match the already-correct sibling call sites in the same file,
and `ctx.syncCorpusFocus` becomes the single writer of the index's `activeScope`.

Purpose: make the D2 (project→docs browsing), D-07 (chapter reveal), and GAP-3 (active-row
fidelity) truths hold for ALL reachable node shapes, not just today's live `recense.db`.
Output: unified recognized-project derivation + null/focus guards + single-writer active state,
with an integration test that pins the server↔client contract using a hub-only project fixture.

Do NOT modify existing plans 61-01..61-14 (all executed). Do NOT touch the GAP-9 floating-panel
work — that is Plan 61-16.
</objective>
