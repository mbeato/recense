Close two code-quality review warnings from 51-REVIEW.md with surgical, fully-autonomous fixes:

Wire `--check` into `pretest` and pin `wabt` exact.

Purpose: Make the SCALE-03 bounds-checking contract symmetric and the reproducibility gate enforced on the normal test path, so SC2's correctness guarantees are defensible.
Output: One-line guard + test in simd-kernel, one lifecycle-hook edit + one exact dependency pin.
