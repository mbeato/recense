<objective>
Make the recall animation HONEST end-to-end. (1) Give D-07 a real data path: both recall emit sites
carry per-seed retrieval score in the `seeds` payload (engine.ts uses the `seedScore` it already
destructures at line 271 but currently discards at line 300; recall/index.ts emits `score:null` per
WR-02). (2) On the client, drive the animation from the real scored 1-hop payload the server emits,
delete the client-side 4-hop adjacency BFS, light EVERY seed at its own score (not just `seeds[0]`),
render hops as subordinate 1-hop activations gated on hop score ALONE, and replace the flat linear
decay with the D-06 attack/hold/exponential-fade envelope. Demote `MAX_HOPS`/`TRACE_FANOUT`/
`TRACE_MAX_EDGES` from content generators to defensive safety caps.

Purpose: every recall pulse becomes true to a node/edge the engine actually scored; the strong top
seeds dominate (brightness ∝ score) and the tail self-attenuates — killing the clutter at its root.
Output: per-seed-score emit at both sites + rewritten `applyTrace` (all-seeds, score-gated hops) +
decay envelope + palette/decay constants + the machine-checkable exactly-N / score→intensity test.

NOTE on scope: the engine emit carries the already-computed `seedScore` into the payload only —
ZERO scoring/traversal/ranking math changes; it is presentation plumbing inside the already
fire-and-forget T-10-05 guard (founder-sanctioned additive change for D-07). The `score × edge.w`
clause of D-07 is dropped on the client: the honest 1-hop payload carries no edge weight and the
hops are a flat union across all seeds' neighbors with no recorded source seed, so brightness is
gated on `score` alone (founder-approved override of D-07's edge.w term — it was unimplementable
from the honest payload without re-deriving adjacency, which is the theater this phase kills).
</objective>
