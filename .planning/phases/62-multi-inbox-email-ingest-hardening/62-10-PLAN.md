<objective>
Remove the net-new attacker capability plan 62-05 introduced. Before 62-05, `event_ts` did not influence
processing order at all. After it, `orderEpisodesForConsolidation` sorts ascending by `event_ts` and this
codebase's reconcile semantics are last-applied-wins — so the maximum `event_ts` in a batch is processed last
and its claim survives. Every genuine message has `Date: <= now`, so a sender who stamps `Date:` 1-47 hours
ahead is *guaranteed* the maximum, and `parseEmailDate` accepts it **unclamped**.

Purpose: closes `62-VERIFICATION.md` gaps[1] and `62-REVIEW.md` CR-03 (BLOCKER). Reproduced by the verifier
against the shipped `dist/` build and re-reproduced during planning of this plan against

`tests/gmail-event-ts.test.ts:88-90` true as written.

> **After the clamp, `parseEmailDate` can never return a value greater than `nowMs`. The maximum ordering
