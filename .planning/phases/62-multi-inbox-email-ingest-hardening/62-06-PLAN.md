Make `tests/backfill-chronological-order.test.ts` actually prove what it claims to prove: that
`orderEpisodesForConsolidation` is wired into the real `consolidate()` pass.

Purpose: closes `62-VERIFICATION.md` `gaps[0]`. The EMAIL-04 production mechanism is verified correct and
is NOT being changed by this plan. The defect is entirely in the test: `DynamicReconcileProvider.generate()`

confirmed this with three independent break/restore cycles against `src/consolidation/consolidator.ts:532`.

written to guard against it.

goes RED against a deliberately-reverted wiring line and GREEN when it is restored.
