<objective>
Capture the two inputs the DRIFT-03 key needs (Gmail's server-assigned `threadId` and the sender-controlled `From:` header), thread the derived key onto `NormalizedRecord`, and close DRIFT-04's first half by ordering a query-backfill batch chronologically before it is appended.

Purpose: `threadId` is already returned by the `messages.get` call the fetcher makes today and is simply discarded — capturing it is wiring, not new I/O, and it is the ONLY trustworthy lineage signal available (D-04 forbids reconstructing lineage from `References`/`In-Reply-To`, which are sender-controlled). The backfill sort closes the most common out-of-order case, a single account's own backlog, without any schema change (research Pitfall 5's lightweight compromise).
Output: `threadId` on `RawGmailMessage`, `provenance_key` on `NormalizedRecord`, a gated derivation call in `normalizeGmailMessage`, a chronological backfill reorder in `pull()`, and adapter-level tests.

**Not in this plan:** no mint-site change and no `countDistinctProvenance` change (65-07), no consolidator wiring (65-08). Nothing downstream reads `provenance_key` yet — after this plan it is carried and observable, and inert.
