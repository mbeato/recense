    Extract the probe's `scan` WAT (49-wasm-simd-probe.cjs lines 35-61) into a standalone `src/retrieval/simd-kernel.wat` and extend it per D-01 so it folds the reciprocal-norm and emits cosine directly in one pass — eliminating the separate scalar norm-divide loop.

    Module shape: `(import "env" "mem" (memory $mem 1))`, export ONE function `scan`. Keep the probe's outer row loop, inner f32x4 accumulate (`f32x4.mul` of `v128.load` query lane × `v128.load` matrix-row lane, `f32x4.add` into the `$acc` v128), 16-byte inner stride (4 floats per step), and the 4-lane horizontal sum (`f32x4.extract_lane` 0..3 added pairwise) producing the raw dot for row `r`.

    Add two inputs so the kernel emits cosine, NOT raw dot: (a) a new param `$rnPtr i32` pointing at a contiguous f32 region of length `count` holding the precomputed PER-ROW RECIPROCAL norm `1/‖row_r‖` (the loader supplies this once, derived from the index's `norms: Float64Array` — do NOT recompute the row norm in WAT, consume the precomputed value per the discretion note); (b) a new param `$recipQNorm f32` = `1/‖q‖` for this query. After the horizontal sum yields the raw dot for row `r`, multiply: `cosine = dot × (f32.load $rnPtr + r*4) × $recipQNorm`, then `f32.store` that into the scores region at `$outPtr + r*4`. Multiplying by precomputed reciprocals (loader pre-divides) is the denom guard: a zero row-norm or zero query-norm becomes reciprocal 0 on the loader side, so the stored score is exactly 0 — matching `cosineSimF32`'s `denom === 0 ? 0` semantics (PATTERNS "Denom guard").

    Final param order: `(param $qPtr i32) (param $mPtr i32) (param $rnPtr i32) (param $outPtr i32) (param $recipQNorm f32) (param $n i32) (param $dimsBytes i32)`. Requires `dim % 4 === 0` (1536 satisfies; the loader guards and falls back otherwise — Plan 02). Do not implement top-k selection in WAT (D-02 keeps selection in JS).
  </action>
