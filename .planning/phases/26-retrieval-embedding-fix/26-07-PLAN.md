src/consolidation/update-decision.ts — routeContradiction(peMagnitude, resistance, config):

  ratio < peReconcileBandLow (0.8)  → 'hold'
  ratio < peReconcileBandHigh (2.0) → 'reconcile'
  ratio >= peReconcileBandHigh AND resistance >= peAppendNewMinResistance (0.3) → 'append-new'
  ratio >= peReconcileBandHigh AND resistance <  peAppendNewMinResistance → 'reconcile'
  PURE function; no DB/clock. isOscillation + countDistinctProvenance also live here.
